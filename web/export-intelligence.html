How to read this dashboard

National figures describe Indonesia's aggregate export performance. Province-of-origin publications provide the appropriate basis for West Sumatra commodity and destination analysis. Export values are not interchangeable with port throughput, supplier capacity, or marketplace sales.

Data coverage displayed: national 2024–2025; West Sumatra 2025 annual trade summary released by BPS on 2 February 2026. Commodity shares and destination values refer to Sumatera Barat exports. Always check BPS revisions and publication notes before using figures in formal reports.
